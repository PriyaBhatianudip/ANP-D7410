A hospital has an emergency department where patients can be added from both ends of the waiting queue:
Normal patients are added at the rear.
Critical patients are added at the front.
The patient at the front is treated first.
After treatment, the patient is removed from the front.

Answer
step 0: create a queue
Deque<String> queue = new Deque<String>();
step1: understand the patient, whether he is a critical patient or normal patient

step2: if that patient is critical, then add him in front of the queue, otherwise add him in the last of the queue.
step3: start the treatment, whoever is at the front of the queue, will go for the treatment.
step4: once the treatment is finished, remove him from the queue
