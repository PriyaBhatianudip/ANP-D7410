1.create a maven project :  Smart Lost & found management system
Project Scenario:-
A college campus receives reports of lost and found items every day. Students can report missing belongings, register found items, search for matching reports, and claim items after verification.
Unlike a conventional Employee or Student Management System, this project gives students a reason to implement multiple entity relationships, search queries, transactions, and business rules.
