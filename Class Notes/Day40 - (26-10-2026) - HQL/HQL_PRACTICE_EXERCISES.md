6. Display students whose names begin with `S`.

8. Display all unique cities.

11. Count students in each department.

13. Display departments whose average marks exceed 75.

15. Use a fetch join to load students and their departments.
16. Display students whose marks are above the overall average using a subquery.
17. Display the first three students using pagination.
18. Update one student's marks using a named parameter.
19. Delete students below a chosen marks threshold (test only with disposable data).
